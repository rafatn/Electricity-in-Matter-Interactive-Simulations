Bus & Truck: Distributed Wheel Energy Recovery + Central Battery + Hydraulic Accumulator

A single-file HTML that formalizes your concept for heavy vehicles (bus / truck): per-wheel modules (generator + local buffer battery + controller), a central energy core, and an optional hydraulic pressure accumulator for stop-and-go routes. This is designed as energy recovery (braking + suspension vibration), not “free energy”.

Recovery channels (what you can realistically harvest)

  • Regenerative braking (primary): convert braking energy into electrical energy (or hydraulic pressure).
  • Regenerative suspension (secondary): harvest from vertical motion/shocks; good on rough roads.
  • Thermal (optional): heat gradients (brake disks / exhaust) via TEG; usually lower yield.
  • Steady rolling harvesting: possible but introduces drag; only makes sense if it replaces existing losses.
Key rule: You can recover energy that would otherwise be dissipated (brakes, suspension damping). If you extract energy continuously from wheel rotation in cruise, you add resistive torque, and the engine must compensate.

3) Compact math model

Let \(m\) be vehicle mass, and a braking event reduces speed from \(v_0\) to \(v_1\) (m/s):

(1) Kinetic energy change: ΔE_k = 1/2 · m · (v_0^2 - v_1^2) (2) Electrical recovered energy per event (overall efficiency η_e): E_e ≈ η_e · max(0, ΔE_k) (3) If split across N wheel modules: E_e,i ≈ w_i · E_e, with Σ w_i = 1 (4) Suspension harvesting (daily) in Wh: E_susp,day (input parameter) (5) Total daily recovered energy: E_day ≈ (E_e · stops_per_day) + E_susp,day

Hydraulic accumulator (gas-charged): idealized energy capacity is roughly \(E_h \approx \int P\,dV\). In practice, it strongly depends on pressure range and gas model. This file treats it as a separate “bucket” you can size.

4) Engineering notes (bus vs truck)

Vehicle What makes recovery strong Recommended configuration
Urban bus Very frequent stops, high mass, predictable routes Max regen braking + hydraulic accumulator + strong balancing
Coach / intercity bus Fewer stops; long cruise segments Regen braking + suspension harvesting + reduce cruise drag harvesting
Delivery truck Stop-and-go, variable loads Regen braking + robust redundancy + per-axle tuning
Highway truck Mostly cruise; long grades/braking on descents Regen for downhill/braking + thermal management + large core
